Breakfast Smoothie

Home

Description

Short on time in the mornings? Not a problem with these quick high-protein smoothies. Make one of them for breakfast and you're good to go.

Ingredients

  • 100g/3½oz fat-free Greek-style yoghurt
  • 1 small banana (75g/2½oz peeled weight), broken up
  • 175ml/6fl oz skimmed milk
  • Pinch ground cinnamon
