Banana Pancakes

Home

Description

These quick and easy banana pancakes are dairy-free and egg-free. Mashed banana is an egg substitute that works really well in pancakes, and makes them taste even better. They can be ready in just 10 minutes, and wolfed down in one! Perfect as a healthy treat for kids.

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas, peeled (approx. 160g/5oz peeled weight)
  • 120g/4½oz plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 120ml/4¼fl oz oat milk (or other plant-based milk)
  • 3 tsp coconut or vegetable oil

To Serve (Optional)

  • Handful blueberries
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • Maple syrup (or honey if you're not vegan), to drizzle
  • Dollop coconut yoghurt
